Recycling househould wastes is a key part of a sustainable development. To increase the scale of recycling, someone thinks that the government should introduce laws to make people recycle waste, instead of relying on the awareness of the public. However, I think it is bad choice.

Pushing people to recycle their waste via punishment is a bad decision. For a resident, it takes extra time and efforts to conduct recycling process, leaving them an unconvenient impression of the recycling. Additionally, forcing people to do recycling might damage personal rights, because it is reasonable for individuals to have rights to decide how to dispose their possessions. Furthermore, the measure also needs state funds to monitor the recycling behaviour of the public, which increases the burden of taxpayers.

Admittedly, driving people to recycle their waste benefits the environment, and develops an environmentally friendly culture. Recycling the waste makes the most of natural resources, reducing carbon emissions and energy consumption. If the society do not recycle waste and decide to cinerate the waste, it will pollute the air, damaging the air quality. If the waste ends up in landfill sites, more land have to be turned into landfill sites, putting pressure on land shortages. Moreover, the measure will possibly raise the environmental awareness of the public, encouraging them to lead an eco-friendly lifestyle. Consequently, an environmentally friendly culture would be possibly established.

Nevertheless, the advantage of the measure is hard to be verified, while the disadvantage of that is so unaffordable. Overall, I think the laws should not to be introduced to make people recycle their waste.
