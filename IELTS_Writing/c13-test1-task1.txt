The maps compares the change of road access to a city hospital between 2007 and 2010.

Overall, the main layout of road system almost remained unchanged over the period, while there was modification of public transport facilities.

Two roundabouts were added to the road system. They were located at the intersection of Ring Road and Hospital Rd, the intersection of Hospital Rd and City Road, respectively. The position of Ring Road, Hospital Rd, and City Road remained unchanged by 2010.

Six bus stops near Hospital Rd were demolished by 2010, making way for a car park and a new bus station. The new bus station was constructed opposite the western side of Hospital Rd, connecting the two roundabouts. The old car park which served both staff and the public only opened to staff. However, a new car park was built on the east of city hospital, becoming a public car park. The entrance of the new public car park lay on its western side, near Ring Road.
