Living in a country where you have to speak a foreign language can cause serious social problems, as well as practical problems. To what extent do you agree or disagree with this statement?
---

It is common to see that there are many people speaking foreign language nowadays. Someone argue that it can cause serious social and practical problem. I do agree with this opinion, and measures should be made to prevent those problems.

Language plays an undoubtly central role in our daily communication. This means that for those who live in foreign country are easily get into trouble without speaking local language, especially in some common situations such as healthcare or business. In these areas, even tiny misunderstanding or communication barrier might cause unafforadable consequence. In this way, people who cannot speak local language tend to face overwhelming difficulties in their daily life.

Using foreign languages is possibly damage community cohesion. For those who can not speak local language, they are likely to misunderstand local people, becoming difficult to socialize or just interact with their neighborhood, and finally feeling a sense of loneliness and isolation. Furthermore, those needy people might turn to people who can speak the same language they can understand rather than local residents. This might cause serious language divide between them and local people. The divide might cause confict or resentment, which might increase the rate of crime.

As a result, speaking foreign languages may lead both social and practical problems. For governments or charitable organizations, helping those people to sharpen their language skills is vital to tackle this issue.
