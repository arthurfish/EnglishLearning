Some people believe that nowadays we have too many choices. To what extent do you agree or disagree with this statement?
---

Since we could easily decide which anime to watch or which podcast to listen, someone argues that we have too many choices today. I aggree with this perspective partly. However, the number of choices about life is not as large as we expected.

Thanks to advanced technology, we could make far more choices online instead of being limited to the physical world. The cyberspace provides so many choice about entertainment or shopping, and in these areas we do have more choice than in the past. For example, we have more shopping choices in online platforms, and we are able to choose which movie to watch or which novel to read. This means that as a consumer we do have more choices than in the past, especially those could be easily accessed online.

On the other hand, we have limited or even fewer choices about our life than the previous generations. The world has been more competitive than before. In the intense competitive world, almost everyone has to put all their energy and time to keep their competitive enough, especially in some developing countries such as China or Souch Korea. Facing greater pressure from workspaces or education systems, people nowadays can difficultly decide their lifestyles , which means that they do not have enough choices on their daily life. If a person has no way to get rid of overwhelming and repetitive daily routine, it is difficult to say that he or she have too many choices.

So the answer is, we do have more choices related to shopping or entertaining, while we do not have enough choices about our life.
