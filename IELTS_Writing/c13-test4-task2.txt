In spite of the advances made in agriculture, many people around the world still go hungry. Why is this the case? What can be done about this problem?
---

Hunger is still a large threat around world, especially in some African underdeveloped countries. Although agricultural advances have been made in these years, many people still live in serious hungry. I think the main cause of this case is about politics, and to tackle the problem other countries should provde more effective international aid.

In these underdevloped and starving countries, politic factors play a central role in agriculture. In these countries, the governments cannot protect private possessions well because of war or greedy and selfish officers. However, agricultural industry needs a stable and safe environment that farmers can invest in their time and effort without worrying about that whether or not their effort would be in vain. While farmers are not motivated, it is easy to expect that there are fewer foods to supply. So the unstable and high-risk environment absolutely damages local agriculture, and finally leading to hunger.

To tackle the issue, other countries could provide more effective international aid to those starving countries to mitigate hunger. However, the measure cannot eliminate the threat. "Effective" means that the they should pay attention to whether or not the aid ending in needy people rather than greedy and selfish officers. If these aid are received by the officers, local people cannot benefit from the aid, and the aid will end up being wasted.

Hunger still exists around the world. Although agricultural technology has been advanced, modern agricultural machines has been made, without a healthy and stable politic environment, starving people cannot benefit from those advancements.
