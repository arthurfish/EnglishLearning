The two maps compare layout of Beechwood Farm in 1950 and the same site today.

Overall, the farm has been converted into a tourist-oreinted attraction over the period. New facilities related to sightseeing have been contructed, while some buildings have experienced slightly change.

The most significant changes appeared in former sheep field. Two new parking lots have been builted in the west and the south corner of the site. In the central area of the former sheep field, a camping field has been erected, with solar panels being situated on its eastern side.

Regarding the rest of the farm, small changes have been made. The former track has been turned into road. The barn made way for holiday cottages and been relocated near a crossroad. A farm shop was built in the area planting fruit trees. However, a farmhouse and a chicken field remained unchanged over the period.
