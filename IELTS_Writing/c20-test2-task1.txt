The maps show how Beechwood Farm changed from 1950 to today. Overall, the most significant change is new built facilities in sheep, while other buildings witnessed a slight change.

First of all, the most surprising change appeared in sheep area. Two new parking has been established in the western and southern corners of the area. Camping field has been built near the parking, situated in the middle of sheep. On the eastern side of camping filed, solar panels were built, closed to a new parking place.

There are other small change which has been made from 1950. Old track turned into road. Barn has been relocated to a cross of roads, its original site was replaced by holiday cottages. A new farm shop has been established near fruit trees, whereas a farmhouse and chickens remained the same.

In general, there are many new facilities built in Beechwood Farm especially in the sheep area, while there are still some tiny changes in other areas being made.
