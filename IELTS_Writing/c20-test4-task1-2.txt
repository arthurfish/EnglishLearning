The diagram illustrates the process of manufacturing fabric from bamboo. 

Overall, it contains nine steps, which could be divided into three main process: growing and harvesting bamboo, processing it to the right size and shape, and weaving the fabirc for the purpose of making clothes.

Initially, bamboo plants are planted in Spring, and then are harvested in Autumn. Following this, the plants are cut into strips. Once the strips have been cut, they are crushed in order to make liquid pulp. After this, the strips go through filters to separate long fibres from liquid. These fibres are soften at the next stage, with water being added and oxide being amined. When the fibres have been ready to the next stage, they are spon to make yarn. After being woven to make fabric, the fabric is finally used to produce clothes.

Going through these stages, the bamboo plants end up being fabric we use in our daily life.
