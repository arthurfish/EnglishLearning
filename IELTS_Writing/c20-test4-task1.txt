The diagram illustrates the process how the bamboo is turned into fabric during about 8 steps. After these operations the bamboo ends up being clothes that we wear everyday.

At the first stage, the bamboo is planted in Spring. Next, people harvest the bamboo in Autumn. As this original material of fabric has been collected, the bamboo is expected to be cut into strips, and people will crush these strips to make liquid pulp.

At the next stage, the crushed strips will go through a filter. The target of this step is to separate long fibres from liquid. After this, the long fibres are filtered while the short ones remains. Next, add water and amine oxide to soften fibres. The fibres are ready to spin now. Then spin them to make yam, and be weaved to make fabric. Finally, the fabirc will be used to make clothes that we wear everyday.
